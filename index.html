Why CrossHealthConnect Exists

After personally experiencing the challenges of seeking treatment abroad and building relationships with healthcare providers, I, Emmanuel Maeke Kioko, created CrossHealthConnect to make the process easier for others.

We understand the struggles African patients face when seeking medical care overseas. Our mission is to provide the guidance and support we wished we had during our own journey.

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to your most common questions

How much does treatment abroad cost?

Costs vary depending on the treatment and destination. Generally, you can save 30-70% compared to local private healthcare. We provide transparent quotes after reviewing your case.

Do you arrange visas?

Yes, we assist with visa applications and provide all necessary documentation to support your medical travel.

Which hospitals do you work with?

We partner with JCI-accredited and internationally recognized hospitals in India, Turkey, UAE, Thailand, and South Africa. View our Hospitals We Refer To section for details.

How long does the process take?

From initial inquiry to treatment, the timeline varies. Simple consultations can be arranged within 1-2 weeks, while complex surgeries may take 4-6 weeks for planning and preparation.

Is there a consultation fee?

Our initial consultation is free. We only charge a service fee if you proceed with treatment through our network, which is clearly disclosed upfront.

Can I get a second opinion before traveling?

Absolutely. We encourage second opinions and can arrange virtual consultations with multiple specialists to help you make an informed decision.
